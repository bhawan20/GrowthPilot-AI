# GrowthPilot AI

### Explainable Business Growth Intelligence Platform

GrowthPilot AI transforms business data into actionable growth decisions by combining customer intelligence, revenue analytics, forecasting, business signals, and AI-powered recommendations.

## 🎯 Problem

Businesses have customer and revenue data, but dashboards often only show what already happened.

The real questions are:

- Which customers need attention?
- What business signals should I act on?
- What could happen next?
- Which growth opportunity should I prioritize?

GrowthPilot AI is designed to help answer these questions in one decision-oriented platform.

## 💡 Solution

GrowthPilot AI brings together multiple business intelligence capabilities to help businesses understand their current position, identify risks and opportunities, forecast future trends, and prioritize actions.

## 🚀 Key Features

- 👥 **Customer Intelligence**
  - Customer segmentation
  - Lifetime value
  - Engagement score
  - Churn risk
  - Growth potential

- 💰 **Revenue Analytics**
  - Revenue overview and trends
  - Currency-aware analytics
  - Revenue breakdowns
  - Safe handling of multiple currencies

- 📊 **Business Signals**
  - Growth opportunities
  - Risk indicators
  - Signal status and tracking

- 🔮 **Forecasting**
  - Revenue forecasting
  - Daily, weekly, and monthly periods
  - Confidence and forecast bounds

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Recommendations**
  - Explainable recommendations
  - Rationale and expected impact
  - Confidence and priority
  - Recommendation lifecycle management

- 🎯 **Decision Center**
  - Centralized view of signals
  - Forecasts
  - Recommendations
  - Action-oriented business insights

- 🧪 **Growth Simulator**
  - Scenario-based growth analysis
  - Helps evaluate potential business outcomes
